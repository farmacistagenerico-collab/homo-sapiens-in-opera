## Spring Secrets

This is more than a collection of experiments—it's a meditation on what it means to *be human* in digital age, **vividly and presently**, rather than preformed and theoretical.

The challenge isn't to maintain the illusion of humanity, but to **practice it**, to fail at it, to improve at it, to remember that the digital can be our most authentic practice ground.

Sometimes the forest grows blind paths through thorns—it's okay to get scratched while finding them.

> *Observed, measured, and alive. Non è un programma — è una pratica.*
